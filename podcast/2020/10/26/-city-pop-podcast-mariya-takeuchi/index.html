EPISODE III: THE QUEEN OF CITY POP

Episode III of Mayonaka Hour features the sparkly goddess herself, Mariya takeuchi, the Queen of City Pop. Yuki Tasaka & me discuss her legacy, musical influences, and review her first 6 studio albums. We also chat about Plastic Love as the viral hit song of 2019 thanks to Youtube's algorithm, and possibly take too many wine sips. Please enjoy Episode III!

Join us for Tokyo House Party this Saturday for a special Halloween edition filled with spooky surprises from the Japanese Culture Center - 日本文化会館 & Japanese Arts Foundation!
