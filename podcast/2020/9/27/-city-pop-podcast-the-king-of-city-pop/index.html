EPISODE II: THE KING OF CITY POP

We're back with another episode of our City Pop Podcast 'Mayonaka Hour.' This time we are discussing the king of City Pop who is none other than Tatsuro Yamashita himself. We talk about him as an artist, his style, and methods for creating the sound that has come to define the genre. Also, we review his first 6 studio albums Circus Town, Spacy, Come Along, Moonglow, Ride On Time, and For You.
