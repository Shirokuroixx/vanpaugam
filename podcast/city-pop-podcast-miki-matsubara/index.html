EPISODE IV: THE HEART OF CITY POP

Episode IV of Mayonaka Hour brings on special guest Allan Ikesaka of Kayo Kyoku Plus featuring the iconic Miki Matsubara; The Heart of City Pop. Allan & me discuss her legacy, musical influences, and review her first 6 studio albums. We also chat about Mayonaka no Door - Stay With Me as a viral hit song thanks to social media platform TikTok. Please enjoy Episode IV!
