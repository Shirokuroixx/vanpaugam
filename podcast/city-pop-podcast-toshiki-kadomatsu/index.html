EPISODE V: THE SOUL OF CITY POP

Episode V of Mayonaka Hour brings on special guest Rocket Brown of Come Along Radio featuring the timeless Toshiki Kadomatsu; The Soul of City Pop. Rocket & me discuss his legacy, musical influences, and review his first 6 studio albums. Please enjoy Episode V!
