Mayonaka Hour 真夜中アワー City Pop Podcast EP VI: Momoko Kikuchi

EPISODE VI: THE PRINCESS OF CITY POP

[This episode is dedicated to Greg Hignight, who tragically passed away October 2022]

Episode VI of Mayonaka Hour brings on special guest Greg Hignight of Tune in Tokyo featuring the peachy Momoko Kikuchi; The Princess of City Pop. Greg & me discuss her legacy, musical influences, and review her first 6 studio albums, including her work with Ra Mu. Please enjoy Episode VI!

Join us for the 3rd Anniversary of City Pop Night on October 16, 2021! With special guest Mika Bridgebook flying in from Tokyo to perform live city pop covers and original music! RSVP by emailing Tokyo@Murasakichicago.com

End theme produced by MIKA Bridgebook & Philadelphia

Mayonaka Hour is hosted by Van Paugam featuring special guests involved in the Japanese music community.

Greg Hignight runs Tune in Tokyo, a club night dedicated to Showa Kayo, K-Pop, Idol music, and City Pop in addition to managing a booking agency for Asian artists seeking live gigs in LA. Check out their IG here: Tune in Tokyo
