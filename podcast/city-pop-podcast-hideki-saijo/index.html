EPISODE VII: THE SPIRIT OF CITY POP

Episode VII of Mayonaka Hour brings on special guest Idolcast of the Idolcast Podcast featuring the wild debut of Hideki Saijo; The Spirit of City Pop. Idolcast & me discuss his legacy, musical influences, and review his first 6 studio albums. Please enjoy Episode VII.

Join us for Tokyo Disko on December 18, 2021! Listen to DJ Van Paugam & Kadobara play City Pop vinyl in one of Chicago’s coolest music venues with a stellar soundsystem! In partnership with Suntory Whiskey.

End theme produced by MIKA Bridgebook & Philadelphia

Mayonaka Hour is hosted by Van Paugam featuring special guests involved in the Japanese music community.

Idolcast is the host of the Idolcast Podcast, a pod dedicated to 60s and 70s Idol, K-Pop, vintage Asian music. Check out their website here: Idolcast Podcast
