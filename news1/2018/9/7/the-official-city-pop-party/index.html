The Official シティポップ 『City Pop』 Party

City Pop: The Lost Genre finally has it’s own dedicated night in Chicago!


Starting Saturday October 20th at 10PM, and every 3rd Saturday of the month experience a live City Pop vinyl DJ set by Van Paugam at the Midwest's finest sake lounge; Murasaki Sake Lounge. 


Re-live Tokyo’s Nightlife in the 1980s during Japan's decadent bubble economy era. Enjoy a wide range of fine Sake, Shochu, Whiskey, and imported Beers from Japan. Listen to authentic vinyl records from the late 70s, and early 80s Japanese music scene including AOR, Light Mellow, and City Pop all night. 

Special thanks to the Japanese Culture Center - 日本文化会館

No Cover - Limited Space. For event details - https://www.facebook.com/events/307438726507885/
