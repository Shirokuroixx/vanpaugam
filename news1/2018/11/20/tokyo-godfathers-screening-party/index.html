Tokyo Godfathers screening + party

2018-11-20

12/14 I’m going to be DJing at the screening + party for Tokyo Godfathers.

Celebrate with The Logan Theatre, the Japanese Culture Center - 日本文化会館 and C2E2 at a Japanese Christmas party and screening of Satoshi Kon's 2003 picture TOKYO GODFATHERS!

8:00 PM - Celebrate in The Logan Lounge with Japanese Arts Foundationresident artist Waking Oni (artist of the stunning "Neo Logan" poster), Van Paugam will DJ vintage Japanese vinyl records including Christmas themed City Pop classics, and Kinokuniya USA will host a pop-up shop of manga items along with holiday stocking-stuffers!

Expect drink specials, raffles, a dance party... and we welcome cosplay!

There is no cover for the Lounge Pre-Party.

11:00 PM - Screening begins. Tickets are $15 and include Waking Oni' limited edition poster, as well as a raffle ticket for prizes from C2E2.

This is an all-ages event. All are welcome!

Details here: https://www.facebook.com/events/943167792555367/
