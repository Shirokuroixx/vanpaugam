Ongaku音楽 every 1st Saturday @ Yokocho

2018-11-30

Every 1st Saturday experience Ongaku音楽, the art of retro Japanese Funk at Yokocho; a new back-alley Japanese Bar in Wicker Park. Take a trip down memory lane as classic records from Japan's bubble economy era set the mood for an authentic experience in an artisanally crafted interior by renowned woodworker Steven Teichelman. 

Enjoy imported beer, sake, and traditional Japanese street fare in an atmosphere that invites you to get lost in a different time and place.
Van Paugam provides the soundtrack to a new kind of immersive experience, spinning vinyl from artists such as Tatsuro Yamashita, Mariya Takeuchi, Toshiki Kadomatsu, Anri, and many more.
