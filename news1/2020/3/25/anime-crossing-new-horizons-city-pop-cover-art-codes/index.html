Animal Crossing: New Horizons - City Pop Cover Art QR Codes

Animal Crossing: New Horizons meets City Pop, Because even having them digitally feels good for some reason. I converted some of my favorite City Pop album cover artwork for use in-game via QR Codes. Even if you can’t afford a $400 Tomoko Aran LP in real life, atleast it can keep your digital avatar company on those long isolated nights. Will update the list as often as I can.
