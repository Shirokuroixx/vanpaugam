SIDE A — 和製ポップス

Japanese City Pop:records, writing,and DJ sets.

Van Paugam is the DJ, writer, and vinyl specialist widely credited with the global revival of Japanese City Pop — over a hundred nights in Chicago, Paris, Berlin, and Tokyo, all of it from original Japanese pressings.
