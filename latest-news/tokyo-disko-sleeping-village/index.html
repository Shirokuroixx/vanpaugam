Tokyo Disko @Sleeping Village

Tokyo Disko features the various upbeat music genres of ’80s Japan, like Disco, Funk, and City Pop, that were married to the economic miracle that made the country a global superpower at the time. The style has been said to give the impression of gazing into the hypnotic neon billboards which crowned the futuristic towering skyscrapers of 1980s Tokyo. By using only authentic vinyl records pressed in that era, DJs Van Paugam and Kadobara simulate the nightlife of metropolitan Japan in its golden age of music and capitalism.

21+
