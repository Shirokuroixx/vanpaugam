シティーポップ: 2-YEAR ANNIVERSARY「Capsule Event」

2020-09-27

Murasaki Sake Lounge & Van Paugam present a special capsule event celebrating 2 Years of City Pop Night! Come for a private evening full of smooth Japanese oldies and City Pop on vinyl where you can send song requests prior to the show! A limited attendee invitation for a night at Murasaki with a small amount of guests for an intimate vinyl DJ set with Van & your favorite staff from Murasaki.

A special commemorative gift and access to a special mix will honor your patronage to the venue, as your support in these difficult times provides much needed relief to the staff and venue so that it may continue to operate in the times to come. While we hope to be open normally at no entry cost in the future, currently we are humbly requesting a fee for this limited time experience.

Your patronage comes with a personal thank you from Van Paugam, free small treats, and honor in supporting Murasaki Sake Lounge during one of the most difficult times in Chicago’s history. Your support is much appreciated, and goes a long way to ensure a future for live music venues and restaurants that operate to provide quality service and entertainment to you always.

We are offering 15 guests an opportunity to experience 2 Years of City Pop Night with Van Paugam within the limitations of social distancing between guest tables, safety precautions between staff, and any added measures needed to ensure a comfortable experience for all attendees. We encourage all guests to come dressed as you please, but would love to see you in your best attire. Limit of 10 guests per table.

Date: Saturday, October 24, 2020
Time Slots Available: 6-8PM & 8:30-10:30PM

Invitation Price: $25

1 Drink Minimum Purchase

1 Special Signed Commemorative Gift Per Guest

For availability please call 312-266-2280 or email tokyo@murasakichicago.com for reservations

Due to COVID-19 safety ordinances, only 15 attendees can be invited to attend each of the 2-hour events during the night. We cannot guarantee availability. We will adhere to all safety precautions mandated by the CDC, State of Illinois, and city of Chicago.
