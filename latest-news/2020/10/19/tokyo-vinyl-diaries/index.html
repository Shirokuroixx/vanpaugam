Tokyo Vinyl Diaries

2020-10-19

Tokyo Vinyl Diaries is an episodic vinyl showcase that features Japanese City Pop in specific themes such as rain, romance, midnight, and many others. Each episode a written entry is created by using the translated lyrics from the songs used to piece together diary page that reflects the mood of the music.
