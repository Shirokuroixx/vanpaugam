Kaoru Akimoto - Wagamama Na Highheels (Van Paugam 2021 Mix)

Kaoru Akimoto’s Cologne Remaster Featuring Van Paugam

Extremely proud and honored to have been invited to remix the iconic Kaoru Akimoto’s Wagamama na High Heels for Victor Entertainment Media Japan’s official remaster of the album.

Please visit this link to find where you can hear it: https://jvcmusic.lnk.to/Cologne_1

This project is an amazing opportunity for me to contribute to the revival of City Pop after so many years of trying. In a way I feel a bit of redemption after many defeats.

https://musicguide.jp/information/29798/

Thank you, Kaoru Akimoto!
