City Pop Night 3-Year Anniversary ft. Mika Bridgebook

Saturday, October 16, 2021.

3-Year Anniversary of City Pop Night at Murasaki Sake Lounge with special guest Mika Bridgebook flying in from Tokyo, singing original songs and City Pop covers! Click here for the FB event page.

Mika Bridgebook has been making music for many years and contributed to anime, including Captain Harlock, and other special collaborations.

Her song “Mirai” is also the closing theme of the podcast Mayonaka Hour! She will be performing the song live and in person.

Show starts at 8:30PM!

Seating not guaranteed without RSVP.

RSVP for your seats by emailing Tokyo@Murasakichicago.com or calling 312-266-2280

$10 Suggested Donation. Free CD with donation!
