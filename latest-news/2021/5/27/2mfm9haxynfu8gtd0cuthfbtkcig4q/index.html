2021-05-27

International City Pop Festival & Fundraiser for stopaapihate.org

Saturday May 29th! An all-star lineup of international DJs, producers, and artists will be celebrating the history of Japanese City Pop, and related styles from Asia! The event will be raising funds for https://stopaapihate.org (100% of donations will benefit the organization)

You can watch free at https://www.twitch.tv/djfact50

Lineup and schedule:

12PM PDT -1PM Your host, DJ FACT.50 - City Pop Revival set (all new 2020/2021 releases)

1-2 Rocket Brown (US - Come Along Radio - Classic City Pop)

2-3 Johnny Quan (US - W/ a set of Indonesian, Thai, Chinese, etc. Pop)

3-4 Space Jams Radio (UK - W/ a set of fresh Future Funk)

4-5 Masa (Japan - Original songs & Q&A with your host)

5-6 Greg Hignight (US - Tune In Tokyo - Classic City Pop)

6-7 Chiyoco (Japan - w/ A set of original music!)

7-8 Epoch (UK - Paradise Killer OST - w/ a video walkthrough by your host)

8-9 Van Paugam (US - Sake Lounge - w/ a surprise poolside set!)

9-10 Opus Science Collective (UK - Debuting brand new City Pop album)

10-11 Nostalgia (S. Korea - w/ a set of Japanese, Korean, & Taiwanese City Pop!)

11-12 Chat Noir (US - Seance - W/ a set of Asian Goth & Post-Punk)

See you at the festival!
