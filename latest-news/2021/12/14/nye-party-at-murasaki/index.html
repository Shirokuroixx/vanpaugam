NYE Party at Murasaki!

SHINNENKAI!

あけましておめでとうございます!

Celebrate 2022 with Murasaki for a very special night featuring live music by Mika Bridgebook and a DJ Set by Van Paugam!

RSVP Required for the performance. Ticket required for entry at 10PM for the final count down...

Silver Ticket grants you guaranteed entry with a champagne toast at Midnight [$25]

Gold Ticket grants your guaranteed entry with 2 free glasses of your choice of sake, shochu, cocktails, beer, mixed drinks or select Japanese whiskey, plus 3 Japanese New Year style Summertime chef’s special tasting plates! Oishii! [$75]

End 2021 with a fun time with staff and friends as we ring in the new year, and enter 2022 together with great memories!

RSVP by emailing tokyo@murasakichicago.com or calling (312) 266-2280

CDC Guidelines will be enforced. Mask required when not eating or drinking.
