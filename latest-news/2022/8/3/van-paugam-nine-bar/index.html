New Residency at Nine Bar Chicago

Every 1st Friday I’ll be DJing at Nine Bar in Chinatown. Located behind Moon Palace Express, and conveniently located near the CTA Red Line Chinatown stop.

I’ll be playing the best of Future Funk, Tech House, Remixes, and new music that matches the mood of the Bladerunner-inspired inferior of Nine Bar.
