Japanese City Pop Vinyl Reissues: Where to Find the Best Japanese Records Online

The global renaissance of 1980s Japanese City Pop shows no signs of slowing down. Whether you’re looking to spin lush disco-funk on your turntable or hunting for that elusive masterpiece to complete your collection, finding authentic pressings can feel like a treasure hunt.

If you are wondering where to source legitimate Japanese vinyl re-issues and vintage imports, we have you covered. We’ve put together a directory linking directly to online storefronts and marketplaces where you can search for these iconic records.

Why Japanese City Pop Vinyl is in High Demand

Born during Japan’s economic boom in the late 1970s and 1980s, City Pop blended Western jazz-fusion, funk, R&B, disco, and AOR into a polished, shimmering sound. Many of these albums were recorded with high-quality studio production, making them rewarding listening on vinyl.

With original vintage pressings often commanding high prices on the secondary market, official Japanese vinyl re-issues can offer remastered audio, faithful artwork, and obi-strip reproductions for collectors worldwide. Mastering, packaging, and included extras vary by edition, so always check the release details.

What You’ll Find on Our Recommended Buying Guide

Finding trusted sources for Japanese imports can be tricky with international shipping costs and bootleg issues. When comparing retailers and individual sellers, look for:

  • Official Licensing: Legitimate Japanese import pressings and official re-issues with a clearly identified label and catalog number.
  • Authentic Packaging: Original artwork, restored gatefolds where applicable, lyric inserts, and iconic obi strips. Check which extras are actually included in the edition or used copy you’re buying.
  • Reliable Grading & Shipping: Clear vinyl and sleeve grading, seller feedback, and secure packaging using sturdy LP mailers for both new and vintage stock.

Marketplace listings are offered by individual sellers; inclusion in the directory is not a guarantee of authenticity, condition, or availability. Read each listing and the seller’s return policy before ordering.

How to Choose Japanese City Pop Records Online

Original pressing or official vinyl reissue?

An original Japanese pressing is a copy from the album’s first release; a reissue is a later edition. For your first City Pop vinyl purchase, compare the label, catalog number, release year, and condition rather than choosing on price alone. Reissues are not automatically all-analog or audiophile editions: check the mastering information supplied by the label.

What is an obi strip?

An obi is the paper band wrapped around many Japanese record sleeves, typically carrying Japanese-language release information. An intact obi can matter to collectors, but its presence alone does not authenticate a pressing. For used records, ask for photographs of the actual sleeve, labels, inserts, and obi.

What should you check before importing vinyl?

Compare the total delivered cost, including international postage and any applicable taxes or import fees. Confirm whether a listing is in stock or a preorder, whether multiple records ship together, and how the shop handles damage or returns. For vintage Japanese vinyl, check the condition of both the record and the sleeve.
