Featured on CNN International

Van Paugam Featured on CNN International

I wanted to take a moment to express my heartfelt gratitude for your support. Your encouragement and belief in me have meant the world to me, and have helped me to achieve so much. Without your support, I would not be where I am today. Heartfelt thanks to CNN International, Kristie Lu Stout, Jadyn Sham, Anri, and everyone who made this video possible.

Thank you for always being there for me, whether through your kind words, your time, or your resources. Your generosity has not gone unnoticed, and I am deeply grateful for everything that you have done for me.

I cannot thank you enough for your support and for believing in me. It means more to me than I can possibly put into words. I will always be grateful for your kindness and your support.

I feel honored at being featured on CNN International. CNN International is a prestigious news organization that is known for its high-quality journalism and global reach. It is watched and read by millions of people around the world, and being featured on the network is a testament to the significance and impact of the world I’ve done at promoting Japanese City Pop music.

With sincere thanks,

Van
