Tokyo House Party Presents: ATOMIC ART

Tokyo House Party Presents ATOMIC ART:


On the 75th anniversary of the bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki, Tokyo House Party is exploring art, activism, and aspiration in our Atomic Age.

Saturday, August 8th - 6-10PM

Program Schedule: 6-7 Conversation with Artist Michael Koerner
7:15 Origami crane folding lesson with artist Ty Yamamoto
7:30-8:30 Guest Scholars Dr. Yuki Miyamoto of DePaul University and Matt Field of the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists
8:30-10 Origami City Pop with Ty Yamamoto and Van Paugam

We livestream our parties from Twitch, and you can find us/subscribe to our channel at www.twitch.tv/tokyohousepartychi

Be sure to RSVP on our website www.TokyoHouseParty.org to receive the streaming links directly to your inbox at 2pm the day of the party!As always, we are here for you - wherever you are.- Team Tokyo House Party *Tokyo House Party is a free, open party to serve our collaborative and new communities. We always welcome donations to Japanese Arts Foundation who hosts and supports us!
