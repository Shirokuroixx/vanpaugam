01 · The book 本

Sixteen years, one chapter each

The book runs from 1975 through 1990, one year per chapter. It treats City Pop as a living timeline: how it emerged, how it shifted, and how it spread through artists, producers, fashion, nightlife and design.

Open it anywhere and you'll find something useful: a deep cut, an artist you have never heard of, or context that changes how you hear a familiar track. It is a guided way in for new listeners and a working reference for collectors and DJs.

02 · The photographs 写真

Photographs by Raul Hudson

Every photograph in the book is by Raul Hudson, made in Tokyo, Osaka and Kyoto in 2015. There are 21 of them, printed full bleed with one consistent grade throughout. They aren't a record of the period. They're a portrait of the place.

The Photo Edition and above also come with a signed photo print.
