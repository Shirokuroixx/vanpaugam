Biography & Archival Impact

Van Paugam is an American DJ and cultural ambassador attributed as a primary figure associated with the rise of 1980s Japanese City Pop music gaining popularity in the West starting in 2016. He was among the first DJs to mix Japanese disco, funk, and synth-pop on digital platforms, igniting an archival movement that brought obscure 1980s Japanese pressings back to international prominence.

Originally from Miami Beach, Paugam moved to Chicago in 2011, undertaking Japanese language studies to deepen his understanding of City Pop lyricism and production history. For his efforts in reviving the genre, he was invited to DJ for the Japanese Consul-General of Chicago, featured on CNN International, and has produced official remixes for JVCKenwood Victor Entertainment.
