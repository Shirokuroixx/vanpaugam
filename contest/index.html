 CITY POP FIT CONTEST REGISTRATION

Please fill out the fields below in order to register for the City Pop Fit Contest at Whistler on Thursday, June 27, 2024.

Please be at the venue by 11:00PM. Winners will be announced at Midnight via Text. All participants will be notified of results. Additional prizes may be won with entry of outfit photograph on social media.

1st Prize: New copy of Tomoko Aran’s Fuyu-Kukan, 1 Japanese Arts Foundation Gala Ticket, Sugoi Sweets Chocolate.

2nd Place: Whistler Cocktail Book, Limited Edition Pin, Plushie, Sugoi Sweets Chocolate.

3rd Place. Whistler Cocktail Book, Sugoi Sweets Chocolate.

The requirements of the contest: Must present a look inspired by City Pop. Vintage garments will be considered for extra points.
